## 🌟 Overview

**Toolio** is a premium desktop application built with Electron, designed specifically to help content creators automate repetitive tasks, manage bulk media, and generate & synchronize AI assets efficiently.

It features a breathtaking, hardware-accelerated user interface with deep theming support (Dark / Light modes), real-time authentication, secure device licensing, and completely silent Over-The-Air (OTA) updates.

---

## 🛠️ Built-In Tools Suite

Toolio is packed with specialized creative and automation tools to speed up your workflow:

### 🎨 1. Google Flow Image Studio
Create stunning, high-definition AI images directly inside Toolio.
* **Multiple AI Models:** Choose from cutting-edge models for photorealism, artistic styles, or rapid generation.
* **4K Ultra HD Upscaling:** Generate native crisp images with built-in 2K and 4K upscaling capabilities.
* **Style & Character Consistency:** Add up to 10 reference images to maintain characters and visual styles consistent across batches.
* **Flexible Ratios:** Full support for landscape (16:9), portrait (9:16), square (1:1), and custom aspect ratios.

### 🎙️ 2. Gemini Text-to-Speech (TTS)
Generate studio-grade voiceovers for your videos, stories, and presentations.
* **30 Natural Voices:** A wide variety of expressive male, female, and versatile voice profiles.
* **Voice Directing:** Customize speaking styles (Newscaster, Whisper, Empathetic, Energetic, and more).
* **Speed & Accents:** Full control over pacing and natural accents (American, British, Australian, etc.).
* **High-Quality Audio:** Export clear, broadcast-ready `.wav` audio files instantly.

### 🔓 3. CapCut Pro Unlocker
Unlock the full potential of CapCut Desktop with a single click.
* **Instant Activation:** Easily access Pro features and VIP assets in real-time.
* **Safe & Seamless:** Works directly in memory without modifying your project files or drafts.

### ⚡ 4. CapCut Automator
Fully automate your video editing pipeline inside CapCut.
* **AI-Powered Synchronization:** Automatically matches generated AI images/videos with your script.
* **Whisper Captions Integration:** Syncs perfectly with generated audio tracks.
* **Hands-free Workflow:** Emulates and controls the CapCut timeline to build complex scenes in seconds.

### 🖌️ 5. Whiteboard Animator
Easily convert any image into a stunning Whiteboard Animation video for faceless channels.
* **18 Hand Styles:** Choose from 18 different hand-with-pen styles, or opt for a clean animation without a hand.
* **Advanced Timing Controls:** Full control over total video duration and end-of-video pause time.
* **Color Reveal Settings:** Customize the exact timing and duration for colors to smoothly appear after drawing finishes.

### 🔢 6. Sequence Checker & Shifter
The ultimate tool for managing AI-generated image or video sequences.
* **Missing Frame Detection:** Instantly scans folders containing thousands of files to find missing sequence numbers.
* **Duplicate Management:** Automatically detects and resolves duplicate frame numbers.
* **Sequence Shifting:** Effortlessly shift entire batches of filenames forward or backward to make room for new frames.

### 🧹 7. Prompt Cleaner
A must-have for AI artists and prompt engineers working with massive datasets.
* **Intelligent Tokenization:** Strips out unwanted characters, repeated prefixes, bullet points, numbers, and emojis from your prompts.
* **Custom Pattern Removal:** Define custom Regex or text patterns to mass-clean text files instantly.
* **Formatting Options:** Formats text files into ready-to-use lists for Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, or Luma.
